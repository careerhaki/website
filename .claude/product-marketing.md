# Product marketing context: CareerHaki

Source of truth: the operating brief in the Claude project "CareerHaki - Site". If this file and the brief disagree, the brief wins.

## What it is

End-to-end career coaching and mentorship for people in tech, delivered 1:1 by working practitioners, from first clarity call through the first 90 days in the new job.

## Who it is for

Recent graduates and candidates with two to three years of experience who want a better role in cloud, data, software, DevOps or security. They are in Canada, the US and India. Most arrive by referral from an alumnus.

## What they feel

They have real experience but cannot present it. They apply widely and hear nothing. They doubt themselves in interviews. They do not know which skills or certifications matter for the role they want.

## The offer

Working name: The Ascent. Three stages: Prepare (clarity, skills, resume, personal brand), Land (applications, mock interviews, offer and negotiation), Thrive (the first 90 days in the role). Students stay until they are hired.

## Why it is different

- It does not end at the offer; it covers the first 90 days on the job.
- Mentors are engineers doing the work today.
- Weekly 1:1 sessions with the founder, a senior enterprise cloud architect and former college professor.

## Confirmed proof

- 120+ professionals hired through CareerHaki.
- 82 in 100 students land a role. Write it as a count, never a bare percentage.
- $25K+ average salary increase. Show as "$" with no currency label.
- Typical time to offer: one to four months.
- 4.9 out of 5 on Topmate.
- About 95% of students come by referral.
- 1,400+ students taught by Nithish at Centennial College (About page only, attributed to him personally).
- Alumni-employer logos are real; label them as places alumni work, never as clients.

## Never say

Anything about price, payment terms, fees or guarantees. No job promise. No invented numbers, quotes or scarcity. Not: "800+ professionals", "90% advancement", "3x faster", "money back", "spots left".

## Voice

Positive and growth-oriented, earned by being specific. Leave the reader more confident in themselves. Write to "you". Avoid revolutionize, transform, unlock, empower, journey.

## Primary call to action

Book a discovery call. Secondary: message on WhatsApp. For employers: request a talent conversation.
